Human cooperation evolved within groups, and it came packaged with hostility to outsiders. Cooperation between species works differently: it is held in place by partner choice and by partners whose fortunes are linked, not by kinship or shared group membership. In this post I ask whether we could select for that kind of cooperation between humans and AIs, and sketch a multi-agent simulation that could test the idea. It is a first draft, posted for comment and criticism.
