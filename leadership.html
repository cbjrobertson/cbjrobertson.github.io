Technical leadership

I work with early-stage companies as a fractional CTO and hands-on AI engineer: setting technical direction, building the first versions of hard systems, and getting them ready for regulated customers.

  • Talk to me about your company

Data science and machine learning

Hands-on modelling, not just oversight: forecasting, classification, speech recognition, and explainable AI, with the evaluation discipline to know whether a model is actually working. I have trained and shipped models from transformer fine-tuning to stacked ensembles.

AI engineering

LLM-based products that hold up in production: retrieval, agents and tool use, fine-tuning data pipelines, and human evaluation, built on cloud infrastructure with proper deployment and monitoring.

Engineering leadership

Hiring and leading engineers, data scientists, and annotators; setting roadmaps and technical priorities; and introducing delivery practices that make work predictable. I have taken one company from prototype to trade sale.

Governance, risk, and compliance

Building systems that regulated buyers can say yes to: safeguarding and governance for AI used with children, NHS-facing products handling clinical data, and roadmaps that clear the compliance barriers blocking enterprise sales in financial services.

Health technology

Clinical AI from research to deployment: speech recognition for children with speech disorders, clinical documentation tools used by NHS therapists, explainable AI for dementia diagnosis, and women’s reproductive health.

Enterprise sales

Supporting founders in the room with technical buyers: translating architecture into answers to security, data, and procurement questions, and turning what enterprise customers need into engineering plans.
