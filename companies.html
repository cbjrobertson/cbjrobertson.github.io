Companies

Alongside research, I have co-founded two applied-AI companies and led the technology at both.

Ogma Therapy

Co-founder and Chief Technology Officer,
2024–2026

Ogma Therapy uses AI to help children with speech, language, and communication needs get access to speech therapy. I led research and engineering from prototype to trade sale.

The central technical problem was speech recognition for children whose speech is disordered. Conventional systems decode against a fixed vocabulary and so “correct” a child’s misarticulations away, which destroys exactly the signal a therapist needs. We built phoneme-level models that preserve it, together with the annotation pipelines that created training data where none existed, and safeguarding layers for automated therapy agents. Ogma was acquired by Saltroad in 2026.

Texture AI

Co-founder and Chief Technology Officer,
2018–2022

Texture AI uses natural language processing to help governments and third-sector organizations communicate more effectively. I co-founded it during my PhD.

I led research and engineering on three products. Texture Generate combines an AutoML ensemble, explainable AI, and generative models to tell writers which words drive performance; live A/B tests through media agencies showed 20–50% gains in click-through. Target, funded by an Innovate UK grant that I led, improved click-through for a public-service campaign by 27% and was shortlisted for Campaign magazine’s Tech for Good award. Pulse is a social-listening platform built on topic models.
